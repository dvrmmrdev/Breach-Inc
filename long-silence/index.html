Breach Inc.

An interactive data breach simulation and security awareness exercise for security, tech and business leaders. You make the decisions through a fictional company's response to a major data breach: the weeks of secrecy before the announcement, the public fallout, and what it costs the people who respond. At the end, it shows how your choices played out, with lessons and questions to take back to your own organisation.

It runs in your browser, with no download, account, adverts or tracking. JavaScript needs to be switched on to play.

Decision log
A work of fiction, inspired by the creator's experience of a major cyber breach. All companies, people, projects and events are invented or composites.
