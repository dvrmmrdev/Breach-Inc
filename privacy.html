Privacy

Breach Inc. collects as little about you as possible. This page explains what is collected, why, and what you can do about it.

It covers breach-inc.com. If your organisation hosts its own copy of the game, your organisation's own privacy policies apply to that copy.

Last updated 13 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Breach Inc. is run by Dave Rimmer, who is responsible for any personal data described here. You can contact me at dave.rimmer@gmail.com.

No cookies or tracking

Breach Inc. doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising or any other tracking. The game doesn't load anything from other websites.

Your saved game

Your progress is saved in your own browser, on your own device, so you can carry on where you left off. It is never sent to me. Restart in the game, or clearing this site's data in your browser, deletes it.

Hosting

Breach Inc. is hosted by Fasthosts. Access logging is turned off for this site, so I don't keep a record of visits or of the IP addresses they come from.

Like any web host, Fasthosts needs your device's IP address to send the pages to you, and may process technical information to run and protect its service. Its privacy notice explains how.

If you email me

If you send feedback or a question by email, I'll receive your email address and anything you include in your message. I use it only to reply to you and to improve the game, and I'll delete it if you ask. Email is handled by Google's Gmail service.

Other websites

Breach Inc. links to LinkedIn, Ko-fi, GitHub, Fasthosts and Creative Commons. If you follow those links, those sites' own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete any personal data I hold about you, or object to how it's used, by emailing me. If you're unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office.
