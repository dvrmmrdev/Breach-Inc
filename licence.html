Using it in your organisation

You're welcome to use Breach Inc. free of charge in your organisation's own security awareness and training programmes. You can share it with your staff, play it together in workshops, and host an unchanged copy for internal use. Two conditions apply:

  • Credit it. Wherever you use it, include a link to breach-inc.com.
  • Use it as it is. Don't change, shorten or adapt the story, text or artwork, and don't present it as anyone else's work.

Hosting your own copy

If you'd rather run Breach Inc. on your own network, for example inside a learning platform or behind your firewall, download the latest version from GitHub. It's a plain web page with no build step or server-side code: put the files on any web server, or open index.html in a browser. The same conditions apply, so please keep the links back to breach-inc.com in place.

Organisation licence

If you want to make Breach Inc. your own, or your organisation needs to pay through an invoice, an organisation licence adds:

  • Internal adaptations. Add your own escalation names, policies and links to your incident playbook, translate it, or package it for your learning platform. The changed version is for your own staff only: keep the credit to breach-inc.com, and don't share or publish it outside your organisation.
  • An invoice. I can invoice your organisation and fill in your supplier forms, so it can be paid from a training budget.
  • An editable facilitator pack. The facilitator guide, in a form you can adapt for your own workshops.

The price depends on the size of your organisation and how you'll use it. Ask for a quote, with roughly how many staff will play it and any changes you have in mind.

Paid courses and other commercial use

If you'd like to include Breach Inc. in a course, product or service that you charge for, such as a paid training course, please get in touch to arrange a licence first.
