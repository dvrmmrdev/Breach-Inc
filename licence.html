Free use in your organisation

You're welcome to use Breach Inc. free of charge in your organisation's own security awareness and training: share it with your staff, play it together in workshops, or link to it from your learning platform. To host it on your own network, download it from GitHub. It's a plain web page that runs on any web server. Two conditions apply:

  • Credit it. Wherever you use it, include a link to breach-inc.com.
  • Use it as it is. Don't change, shorten or adapt the story, text or artwork, and don't present it as anyone else's work.

The free version doesn't record who has played it. If it's useful to you, you can support it on Ko-fi.
