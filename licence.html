Using it in your organisation

You're welcome to use Breach Inc. free of charge in your organisation's own security awareness and training programmes. You can share it with your staff, play it together in workshops, and host an unchanged copy for internal use. Two conditions apply:

  • Credit it. Wherever you use it, include a link to breach-inc.com.
  • Use it as it is. Don't change, shorten or adapt the story, text or artwork, and don't present it as anyone else's work.

Hosting your own copy

If you'd rather run Breach Inc. on your own network, for example on your intranet or behind your firewall, download the latest version from GitHub. It's a plain web page with no build step or server-side code: put the files on any web server, or open index.html in a browser. You can also link to it, or to your unchanged copy, from your learning platform. The same conditions apply, so please keep the links back to breach-inc.com in place.

The free version doesn't record who has played it. A learning platform package that records completions is part of the organisation licence.

Organisation licence

An organisation licence is for teams who need evidence of completion, want to make Breach Inc. their own, or need to pay from a training budget. Every licence includes:

  • A learning platform package. A SCORM 1.2 package for your learning management system, which records each player's completion, so you can show who has done the training at audit time.
  • A copy for your organisation. A version without the Ko-fi links or feedback to me, with player feedback going to an address you choose. The credit to breach-inc.com stays.
